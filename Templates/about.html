ABOUT US

Motic Africa

Motic Africa is a dynamic adventure, events, culture and lifestyle company based in Nairobi, Kenya — dedicated to celebrating authentic African flame culture, community and entertainment through premium, family-friendly experiences that bring together adventure, food, music and culture.

We started as a small team passionate about travelling for fun. During camping trips we introduced a barbecue grill competition between teams — and what began as backyard Choma meet-ups supporting local grill masters has grown into a national vision: Kenya's biggest, most authentic celebration of Nyama Choma.

The name MOTIC stands for Modern Techniques, Innovation and Culture — we blend traditional African culinary heritage with modern event experiences.
