We Are MoticAfrica

MoticAfrica is an entertainment and experience platform built to celebrate the people, food, culture and creativity that make Africa special.

We bring people together through exciting events, competitions, music, food and entertainment. Our goal is simple — create experiences where people can have fun, connect with others and create memories.

At the heart of MoticAfrica is the belief that great experiences are better when shared.
